## Claims deliberately excluded or downgraded

- **“The Telegram group was run by the old administrators.”** Unverified. The Postal Police official explicitly cautioned against this inference.
- **“The Telegram group was deleted/restricted in early November 2026.”** Not established in the sources above.
- **“The new group is the same network.”** Too broad. Similar reported conduct and reuse of a name do not prove common control.
- **“The legacy site was founded in exactly 2005.”** Not established by this evidence set. Use “reported active for at least 20 years” unless a primary dated record is found.
- **Any claim of criminal guilt by a named individual.** Not made by this report; preserve procedural status and source date.

## Update protocol

For every new source:
1. Save its direct URL, publisher, author (if stated), publication date and access date.
2. Quote only the minimum necessary; paraphrase with attribution.
3. Record the exact claim it supports and its limits.
4. Search for independent corroboration and contradictory reporting.
5. Update confidence without erasing earlier snapshots.
6. Never add victim names, intimate images, private group links, personal addresses, phone numbers or speculative account attribution.

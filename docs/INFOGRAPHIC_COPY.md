## Headline

# PHICA: A DIGITAL ABUSE CASE — WHAT IS KNOWN, WHAT IS NOT

### A public-source timeline of the legacy websites and the 2026 Telegram group

## Panel 1 — The legacy websites

**2005–2025 (reported operating period)**  
Media described Phica as a long-running forum where images of women were reportedly shared without consent and accompanied by degrading or sexualised comments.

**11–12 SEPTEMBER 2025**  
Phica.net and Phica.eu were seized by the Italian Postal Police under an order from the Rome Prosecutor's Office.

**Evidence:** ANSA (12 Sep 2025); la Repubblica (11 Sep 2025).

## Panel 2 — The Telegram reappearance

**30 OCTOBER – 2 NOVEMBER 2026**  
Italian media reported a Telegram group using the name “Phica – La rinascita”.

**11,159 → 11,240**  
la Repubblica reported these membership snapshots at noon and two hours later on 2 November 2026. Open reported 11,308 at 17:00.

*These are dated journalistic observations, not a current member count.*

## Panel 3 — The critical distinction

<big> SAME NAME ≠ SAME OPERATORS </big>

On 5 November 2026, a senior Italian Postal Police official told Open that reusing a known community name does not prove that the same administrators are behind a new group.

**Public evidence reviewed does not establish operational continuity between the seized websites and the Telegram group.**

This is the central unresolved attribution question.

## Panel 4 — Evidence status

- **CONFIRMED BY MULTIPLE REPORTS:** seizure of Phica.net / Phica.eu in September 2025.
- **REPORTED BY MULTIPLE OUTLETS:** Telegram group using the Phica name in late October / early November 2026.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** same administrators, owners, infrastructure or controlling entity.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** a verified deletion or restriction date for the Telegram group.

## Panel 5 — If your image was shared without consent

**You are not to blame.**

1. Preserve URLs, usernames, dates, messages and contextual screenshots.
2. Do not forward or repost intimate images.
3. Do not pay, negotiate or confront suspected operators.
4. Report to the Italian Postal Police or another law-enforcement office.

## Visual and ethical rules

- No intimate imagery, victim photographs, screenshots from abusive groups, usernames, invite links or identifying details.
- No visual network diagram implying that the legacy site's administrators control the Telegram group.
- Use a split timeline with a clear evidence boundary between 2025 and 2026.
- Show “same name ≠ same operators” as the main analytical finding.
- Label membership figures with exact dates and observation times.
- Use accessible contrast, readable type and no flashing, strobing or rapid animation.
- Add alt text and a plain-text source list in every publication.

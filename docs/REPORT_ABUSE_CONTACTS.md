# Report Abuse — International Help & Official Reporting Channels

**Review date:** 20 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Help victims and witnesses find official reporting, emergency and support channels. Contact details can change; verify the linked official page before relying on a number, especially when reporting from abroad.

> **Emergency first:** If someone is in immediate danger, call the emergency number for the country where they are located. These contacts are not interchangeable: a support helpline, a platform takedown request and a police report serve different purposes.

## 1. Italy

| Need | Contact | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate danger / crime in progress | **112** | Emergency services. Use when there is immediate danger or urgent need for police assistance. |
| Report online abuse / cybercrime | [Polizia Postale — Commissariato di P.S. online](https://www.commissariatodips.it/) | Official information and reporting route for online crimes. For a formal complaint, follow the instructions of the competent police office; an online report does not always replace an in-person statement. |
| Intimate images at risk of non-consensual publication | [Garante Privacy — Revenge porn / Article 144-bis](https://www.garanteprivacy.it/it/temi/revengeporn) | Urgent procedure for a person who has a well-founded reason to fear that private sexually explicit images or recordings concerning them may be distributed without consent. The Garante states that it decides within 48 hours of receiving a qualifying report, subject to the legal procedure. Use the official online form and instructions. |
| Gender-based violence or stalking support | **1522** — [1522.eu](https://www.1522.eu/) | Free, 24/7 national public-service helpline, with multilingual support and referral to anti-violence services. It is a support and orientation service, not a substitute for an emergency call or criminal complaint. |
| Child or adolescent in danger / online material harming minors | **114** — [114.it](https://www.114.it/) | Free, 24/7 child-emergency service. Reports can also be made through its website, chat and app; the site accepts reports about illegal or harmful material involving children and adolescents. |
| Trafficking or severe exploitation | **800 290 290** | Italian national anti-trafficking helpline. Verify current access and service information through official government resources. |

**Important Italy note:** Article 144-bis of the Italian Privacy Code is a preventive route to the Garante for qualifying intimate-image cases. It is not the same as filing a criminal complaint with law enforcement. A victim may need both routes.

## 2. United States

| Need | Contact | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate danger | **911** | Emergency services in the United States. |
| Internet-enabled crime | [FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)](https://www.ic3.gov/) | Online reporting of internet crime. IC3 is not an emergency service; contact local law enforcement for immediate threats. |
| Non-consensual intimate images / image-based abuse support | **844-878-2274 (844-878-CCRI)** — [Cyber Civil Rights Initiative](https://cybercivilrights.org/) | CCRI Image Abuse Helpline and safety resources. Check the official site for current hours and eligibility. |
| Sexual violence support | **1-800-656-4673** — [RAINN](https://rainn.org/) | National Sexual Assault Hotline and online support. |
| Child sexual exploitation | [NCMEC CyberTipline](https://report.cybertip.org/) / **1-800-843-5678** | Report suspected online child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children. |
| Images created when the person was under 18 | [NCMEC Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/) | Free service that can help limit the online sharing of eligible nude, partially nude or sexually explicit images taken when the person was under 18. It uses a hash generated on the person's device; the image is not uploaded to the service. Only participating public or unencrypted platforms are covered. |

The U.S. Department of Justice also publishes information on sharing intimate images without consent and support resources: [DOJ — Know Your Rights](https://www.justice.gov/ovw/sharing-intimate-images-without-consent-know-your-rights).

## 4. International and cross-border reporting

- **European Union / Europe:** Call **112** for an emergency in a country where it operates. For child sexual coercion or extortion, consult [Europol's country-by-country law-enforcement reporting channels](https://www.europol.europa.eu/report-a-crime/law-enforcement-reporting-channels-child-sexual-coercion-and-extortion). The correct non-emergency channel depends on the country.
- **Child sexual abuse material (CSAM):** Use the [INHOPE hotline directory](https://inhope.org/what-we-do/how-to-report) to find the national hotline. INHOPE advises reporting the webpage URL and not forwarding or emailing the material. Do not send CSAM files to INHOPE itself.
- **International child-exploitation reporting:** [International Centre for Missing & Exploited Children — Global Hotline Directory](https://www.icmec.org/reporting-mechanisms/). The directory lists national reporting mechanisms and specialist resources.

## 5. Platform reporting and content removal

Where content appears, use the platform's own report function for non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual exploitation, impersonation, threats or doxxing. Save the content URL, account handle, date/time and report reference where safe to do so.

Platform reporting is **not** a substitute for reporting a crime to law enforcement or seeking specialist victim support. Do not post the material again to draw attention to it.

For eligible images, additional removal tools may be available:
- [StopNCII.org](https://stopncii.org/) — adults can create a hash on their device for eligible intimate images and videos, helping participating platforms detect matching uploads. It does not remove content from the entire internet.
- [NCMEC Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/) — for images taken when the person was under 18, including when they are now an adult. Do not download or share an image to use this service; follow its on-device instructions.

## 6. Safety and evidence checklist

1. If there is immediate danger, contact emergency services first.
2. Preserve URLs, usernames, timestamps, messages, threats, payment demands and platform report IDs where safe.
3. Do not forward, repost or download intimate images to build a public evidence archive.
4. If a minor may be depicted, do not save, screenshot, forward or upload the sexual image. Record the URL and report it through the appropriate official channel.
5. Do not pay extortion demands or confront suspected perpetrators.
6. Secure email and social accounts, review active sessions and enable multi-factor authentication, unless doing so could alert an abuser and increase danger.
7. Ask for a trusted person, victim-support worker or interpreter to assist with reporting if needed.

## 7. Scope and maintenance

This directory is a public-interest signposting resource, not legal advice and not a guarantee that every listed service can investigate every offence. Jurisdiction, age, residence, language and the type of content can affect eligibility and procedure.

**Maintenance rule:** Before each update or publication, check the official source for the number, operating hours, eligibility, geographic coverage and online reporting URL. Record the date checked. Do not copy a phone number from an unofficial directory if an official source is available.

**Victims first. No reposting. No public identification.**
